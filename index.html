Spring Roll Recipe

Description

Spring rolls are a popular appetizer in many Asian cuisines. They are typically made with a thin pastry wrapper filled with a variety of ingredients, such
as vegetables, meat, or seafood.The rolls are then deep-fried or baked until crispy and golden brown.

Preparation Time

  • Total Time : 30 minutes
  • Preparation Time : 15 minutes
  • Cooking Time : 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 10 spring roll wrappers
  • 1 cabbage (shredded)
  • 1 carrot (julienned)
  • 1 capsicum (thinly sliced)
  • 2 spring onions (chopped)
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 teaspoon soy sauce
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Oil for deep frying

Instructions

  1. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a pan.
  2. Add garlic and sauté for 1 minute.
  3. Add cabbage, carrot, capsicum, and spring onions.
  4. Stir-fry for 3–4 minutes.
  5. Add soy sauce, salt, and black pepper. Mix well and let the filling cool.
  6. Place a spring roll wrapper on a flat surface.
  7. Add 2 tablespoons of filling, fold the sides, and roll tightly.
  8. Seal the edge with a little water and flour paste.
  9. Heat oil in a deep pan and fry the spring rolls until golden brown and crispy.
  10. Drain on paper towels and serve hot with ketchup or chili sauce.

Servings

This recipe makes approximately 10 spring rolls and it can serve approximately 3 persons, depending on the size of the wrappers and the amount of filling used
the serving can be more or less.

Tips

  • You can customize the filling by adding cooked chicken, shrimp, or tofu for a non-vegetarian version.
  • Make sure to seal the spring rolls properly to prevent the filling from spilling out during frying.
  • Serve with your favorite dipping sauce for added flavor.
